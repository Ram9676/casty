## ✨ Features
- **Multilingual:** Supports English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and many more.
- **Customizable UI:** Switch themes and UI styles seamlessly.
- **Audio Visualizer:** Dynamic visualizer on the player with various effects.
- **Discover:** News, mood, musical genres, and new albums from your favorite artists.
- **Playlists:** Import/Export locally and online. Share playlists easily.
- **Lyrics:** Fetch, display, edit, and translate synchronized lyrics.
- **Advanced Audio Control:** Volume, playback speed, pitch, normalization, and skip silence.
- **Platform Support:** Android Auto, Android TV, and widgets.
